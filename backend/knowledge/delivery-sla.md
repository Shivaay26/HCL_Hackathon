# Standard Delivery

Standard delivery for in-stock items is 3 to 5 business days inside the service region.

# Express Delivery

Express delivery is 1 to 2 business days and is included for Premium members. Prime members can purchase express delivery at checkout.

# Delayed Shipments

If a shipped order misses the estimated delivery date by more than 2 business days, contact support with the order number. The shipment timeline is informational and does not extend the return window by itself.
