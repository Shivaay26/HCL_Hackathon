# Smartphones

The warranty period of a smartphone is 12 months from the delivery date. The manufacturer warranty covers manufacturing defects. Physical damage and liquid damage are excluded.

# Laptops

Laptops include a 12-month limited warranty covering hardware defects. Battery coverage is 6 months.

# How To Claim

Warranty claims require the order number and product serial number. ShopSmart does not replace accessories under the device warranty.
